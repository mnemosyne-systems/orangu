# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability, please report it to us via an email to the lead maintainer [Jesper Pedersen](mailto:jesper.pedersen@mnemosyne-systems.ai).

> **Note:** This is strictly for reporting security-related issues. Non-security vulnerabilities or general bug reports will not be addressed here.

We sincerely appreciate your responsible disclosure and your contribution to helping us keep our project secure.
